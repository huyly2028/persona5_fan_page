Persona 5

What is Persona 5?

Persona 5 is a role-playing game by Atlus, first released in 2016. You play a high school student who moves to Tokyo and lives a double life. By day you go to class, work part-time jobs and build friendships. By night you lead the Phantom Thieves of Hearts, a group that enters the twisted inner worlds of corrupt adults to steal their hearts and make them confess their crimes. The game mixes turn-based combat, life-sim elements and a stylish jazz-infused soundtrack.

How to Get Started

  1. Pick the right version. Persona 5 Royal is the definitive edition, with a new character, a new semester and many quality-of-life improvements.
  2. Choose your platform. Royal is on:
    • PlayStation 4 and PlayStation 5
    • Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S
    • Nintendo Switch
    • PC (Steam and Windows)
  3. Pick a difficulty. Normal suits most players. Choose Easy or Safe if you mainly want the story.
  4. Plan your days. Time is limited, so balance Palace dungeons with schoolwork, part-time jobs and Confidant relationships.
  5. Don't rush. A full playthrough runs 100+ hours, so enjoy the music and the story.

A personal note: Royal may be the definitive edition, but I played the original Persona 5 and fell in love with it. You can't go wrong with either version.

My Connection

I picked it up in fall/winter 2021 and absolutely fell in love with the plot, characters, gameplay, artwork, and soundtrack. I have always loved anime since I was young but never found a game that so perfectly encapsulates the feeling of a slice of life anime while still providing agency and fantasy for the player. Maybe it was during covid that I was not able to have a normal freshman experience and lived vicariously through Joker and the friendships he made during his journey through the game. I bought the original Persona 5 because it was on sale for $7.99 and realized soon after I bought it that it was the best $8 I've ever spent because over the next few weeks, I sank over 100 hours into the game without realizing it as I played invested and enthralled in the next story development. Anyways, this website is my love letter to the game and I still listen to the tracks that lulled me to sleep during those late night play sessions.
